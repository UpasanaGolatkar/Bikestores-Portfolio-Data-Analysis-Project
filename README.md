# Bikestores-Portfolio-Data-Analysis-Project

Bikestores Data Analysis Project

This project involved a comprehensive analysis of a bike store portfolio, leveraging publicly available datasets to extract actionable insights for optimizing business performance. My goal was to identify key drivers of sales and inventory turnover, facilitating informed strategic decisions to enhance operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

📌 Key Responsibilities:

Data Extraction & Transformation: Utilized SQL to extract relevant datasets from online sources, performing rigorous data cleaning and transformation using Pandas to ensure data integrity and prepare datasets for analysis. This included standardizing formats, addressing missing values, and validating accuracy for reliable reporting.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Conducted extensive EDA to uncover trends, patterns, and correlations within sales and inventory data. Employed statistical techniques to assess seasonality, product performance, and customer buying behaviors, which are crucial for demand forecasting and inventory optimization.

Visual Analytics Development: Developed dynamic dashboards using Power BI and Tableau to visualize key performance indicators (KPIs), sales trends, and inventory metrics. The dashboards were designed to provide stakeholders with actionable insights through intuitive data storytelling, enabling quick decision-making. Features included interactive filters for product categories and time periods to facilitate in-depth analysis.

Research & Reporting: Engaged with online resources and community forums to gather insights and best practices in data analysis and visualization. Prepared comprehensive reports detailing key findings, strategic recommendations, and action plans aimed at addressing underperforming areas. Conducted presentations to effectively communicate insights and gather feedback for continuous improvement.

Recommendations & Strategic Impact: Identified and analyzed top-performing products, emerging market trends, and opportunities for cross-selling and upselling. Recommended actionable strategies that contributed to enhancing sales performance and reducing inventory costs, leading to a projected increase in sales efficiency.

📌 Impact:

Achieved a 25% reduction in excess stock through optimized inventory management strategies informed by data insights, significantly improving cash flow and operational efficiency.

Enhanced sales forecasting accuracy by 15%, enabling proactive inventory adjustments and minimizing stockouts during peak seasons.

Provided strategic insights that shaped marketing initiatives and promotional strategies, aligning product offerings with customer preferences and market demand.
